Who are We

Our story

Hello, we are FlyIST, a high school team that has been competing in technology competitions for the past two years, mainly in general aviation and space. We placed 7th nationally in both Teknofest’s High School Rocket and UAV competitions in 2025. We also took part in the NASA Space Apps Challenge last year, where we worked on the “Meteor Madness” challenge. This year, we returned to Space Apps and are now taking on “Be an Earth System Trend Detective!”, using NASA Earth observation data to uncover how our planet is changing.

What is our project

EarthTREND is a globe of NASA Earth data from 1990 to 2050. Pick air temperature, rain, soil wetness or sunlight, pick a country, and it shows what changed, where, by how much, and whether scientists would call the change significant.

Every trend gets the test scientists use, the Mann-Kendall test, corrected for runs of similar years. The years to 2050 come from a forecast that keeps the level of the last 20 years and a trend pooled with neighbouring areas. Tested on 2017 to 2025, years it never saw, it beat a plain straight line for temperature and rain.

For harvests, a crop detector gives each country's chance of a poor harvest next season and shows every bad year since 1986. It is honest about its reach: it knows where shortfalls are common, not when the next one comes.

Data: NASA POWER (MERRA-2 and CERES), NASA GIBS, FAOSTAT, Natural Earth.
